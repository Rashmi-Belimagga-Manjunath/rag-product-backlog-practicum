# Tally social acknowledgement and sharing

Document: DOC-PRD-T-04-v2
Status: current. Version 2.0. Last modified 2026-07-18.

Author: Founder
Product: Tally (PROD-T)

## Background

Covers badges and challenges, and the explicit, revocable sharing of habits.

## Goals

Reduce the friction between signing up and keeping a first habit going, while keeping the streak mechanic predictable and honest for every user.

## Non-goals

This document does not cover habit template curation or social features. Those are specified separately.

## Requirements

### REQ-T-06: Progress feels acknowledged

Milestones and progress must be surfaced to the user in a way that acknowledges sustained effort.

This requirement is normative for Tally. Where another document disagrees, this statement takes precedence.

### REQ-T-07: Sharing is explicit and revocable

Any sharing of habits with others must be explicit on the user's part and reversible at any time.

This requirement is normative for Tally. Where another document disagrees, this statement takes precedence.

## Success measures

Measured as: thirty-day streak survival, share of recovery prompts accepted, and repeat-week retention after a prompt is offered.

## Open questions

How forceful should the first recovery prompt be? The current answer of a single light suggestion is provisional.
