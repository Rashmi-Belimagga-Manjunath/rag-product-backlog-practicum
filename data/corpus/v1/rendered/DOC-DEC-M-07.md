# Decision DEC-M-07

Document: DOC-DEC-M-07
Decided: 2026-06-30
Decided by: Finance lead

## Decision

Reporting reflects only approved hours from the ledger.

## Rationale

Finance reconciliation is only trustworthy when unapproved hours are excluded.

## Applies to

FEAT-M-05 (Approved Hours Reporting).

## Context

The recommendation was weighed against the current delivery backlog; the owning team will revisit it at the next quarterly review if the underlying cost model shifts.
