# Meridian finance reporting from the ledger

Document: DOC-PRD-M-03-v2
Status: current. Version 2.0. Last modified 2026-07-18.

Author: Product Manager
Product: Meridian (PROD-M)

## Background

Fixes the ledger as the single source for approved hours feeding finance reporting.

## Goals

Reduce the friction between signing up and keeping a first habit going, while keeping the streak mechanic predictable and honest for every user.

## Non-goals

This document does not cover habit template curation or social features. Those are specified separately.

## Requirements

### REQ-M-05: Approved hours feed finance reporting

Finance reporting must reflect only approved hours from the ledger.

This requirement is normative for Meridian. Where another document disagrees, this statement takes precedence.

## Success measures

Measured as: thirty-day streak survival, share of recovery prompts accepted, and repeat-week retention after a prompt is offered.

## Change log

Version 2.0 supersedes version 1.0. The changes follow the decisions recorded in the decision log and replace the behaviour described in the earlier version.

## Open questions
