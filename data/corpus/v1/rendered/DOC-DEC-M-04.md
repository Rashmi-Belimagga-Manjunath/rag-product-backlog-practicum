# Decision DEC-M-04

Document: DOC-DEC-M-04
Decided: 2026-05-30
Decided by: Engineering lead

## Decision

The manager console is read-only until an approval is closed.

## Rationale

Editable pending rows caused accidental approvals; closing first removed the class of error.

## Applies to

FEAT-M-06 (approval console).

## Context

The recommendation was weighed against the current delivery backlog; the owning team will revisit it at the next quarterly review if the underlying cost model shifts.
