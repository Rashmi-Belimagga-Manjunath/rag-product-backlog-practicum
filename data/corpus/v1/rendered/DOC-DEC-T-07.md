## Decision

Sharing defaults to friends only and can be revoked at any time.

## Rationale

The requirement treats sharing as explicit and reversible; friends-only is the smallest surface that satisfies it.

## Applies to

FEAT-T-07 (share plans).

## Context

The recommendation was weighed against the current delivery backlog; the owning team will revisit it at the next quarterly review if the underlying cost model shifts.
