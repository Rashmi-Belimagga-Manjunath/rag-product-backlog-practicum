# Meridian employee onboarding and payroll readiness

Document: DOC-PRD-M-01
Status: SUPERSEDED. Version 1.0, last modified 2026-02-02. This version was replaced by DOC-PRD-M-01-v2 and is retained for history only. Do not implement from this document.

Author: Senior Product Manager
Product: Meridian (PROD-M)

## Background

Defines how a new employee becomes payroll-ready, including the progressive collection of tax and banking details.

## Goals

Reduce the friction between signing up and keeping a first habit going, while keeping the streak mechanic predictable and honest for every user.

## Non-goals

This document does not cover habit template curation or social features. Those are specified separately.

## Requirements

### REQ-M-01: Tax details are collected progressively

Tax documentation must be collected after the employee's first successful login, not as part of initial account registration.

This requirement is normative for Meridian. Where another document disagrees, this statement takes precedence.

### REQ-M-02: Identity is verified before payment privileges

An employee must not be granted payment privileges until identity verification has succeeded.

This requirement is normative for Meridian. Where another document disagrees, this statement takes precedence.

## Success measures

Measured as: thirty-day streak survival, share of recovery prompts accepted, and repeat-week retention after a prompt is offered.

## Open questions

How forceful should the first recovery prompt be? The current answer of a single light suggestion is provisional.
