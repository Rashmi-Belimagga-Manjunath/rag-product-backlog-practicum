# Decision DEC-M-08

Document: DOC-DEC-M-08
Decided: 2026-08-20
Decided by: Product Manager

## Decision

Benefits enrolment requires a verified identity first.

## Rationale

Unverified enrolment produced duplicate perks. Verification is mandatory before any benefits are applied.

## Applies to

FEAT-M-07 (Benefits Enrolment Portal).

## Context

The recommendation was weighed against the current delivery backlog; the owning team will revisit it at the next quarterly review if the underlying cost model shifts.
