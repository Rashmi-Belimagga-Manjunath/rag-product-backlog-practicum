# Decision DEC-M-03

Document: DOC-DEC-M-03

## Decision

Export payroll files nightly at a fixed clock time; never on demand.

## Rationale

A pre-configured schedule keeps provider feeds predictable and matches the normative requirement.

## Applies to

FEAT-M-04 (automated payroll file).

## Context

The recommendation was weighed against the current delivery backlog; the owning team will revisit it at the next quarterly review if the underlying cost model shifts.
