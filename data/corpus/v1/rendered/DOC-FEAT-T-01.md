## Purpose

Suggests a reduced-effort alternative habit when a user is about to break a multi-week streak.

## Terminology

Within this document the feature is referred to as: Streak Recovery Suggestion Engine.
Users describe the same thing as: streak saver, saving a broken streak, losing a streak.
