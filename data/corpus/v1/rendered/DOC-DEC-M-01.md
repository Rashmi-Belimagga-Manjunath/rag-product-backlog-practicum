# Decision DEC-M-01

Document: DOC-DEC-M-01
Decided: 2026-07-16
Decided by: Senior Product Manager

## Decision

Move tax collection to day three of the onboarding ramp rather than during sign-up.

## Rationale

Early testing showed the long sign-up form caused abandonment in the first session. Asking once the user has seen value raised tax-step completion.

## Applies to

FEAT-M-01 (onboarding ramp).

## Context

The recommendation was weighed against the current delivery backlog; the owning team will revisit it at the next quarterly review if the underlying cost model shifts.
