# Decision DEC-M-05

Document: DOC-DEC-M-05
Decided: 2026-07-02
Decided by: Security lead

## Decision

Payslips are viewable on demand in the app; no payslip email delivery.

## Rationale

Emails with payslip attachments failed security review. On-demand access meets the self-service requirement.

## Applies to

FEAT-M-09 (payslip viewer).

## Context

The recommendation was weighed against the current delivery backlog; the owning team will revisit it at the next quarterly review if the underlying cost model shifts.
