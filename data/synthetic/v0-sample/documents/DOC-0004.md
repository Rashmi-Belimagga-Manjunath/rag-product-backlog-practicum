# Progressive Profiling - Technical Specification

Feature ID: FEAT-A-01 | Status: in_progress | Priority: 1 | Product: Meridian
Owner: Meridian product team | Product maturity: formal

Summary: Collect profile attributes across the first several sessions instead of requesting everything on the first screen.

## Terminology

In this document the feature is referred to as 'Progressive Profiling'. Alternate phrasing seen elsewhere in the repository includes: onboarding ramp, new-user activation, first-run setup.

## Behaviour

The system exposes Progressive Profiling to the tenant administrator. Behaviour is governed by the linked backlog items below.

## Linked backlog items

- PBI-A-011: Ask for tax details during the first session - status in_progress, 5 points, raised 2026-07-14 by Product Owner.
- PBI-A-012: Save partial profile when a user abandons the session - status to_do, 8 points, raised 2026-08-02 by Product Owner.

## Reported issues

- TKT-104 (high): New signups get asked for tax details straight away and a lot of them never come back. - reported by Customer Success Manager on 2026-08-25.

## Technical dependencies

- DEP-001: Progressive Profiling depends on the Identity Service for session-scoped tenant context.
- DEP-004: Bulk Timesheet Approval depends on Progressive Profiling, because the approval screen shows profile completeness and cannot be built before the ramp exists.
