# Streak Rescue - Technical Specification

Feature ID: FEAT-B-01 | Status: in_progress | Priority: 1 | Product: Tally
Owner: Tally product team | Product maturity: informal

Summary: Detect a streak that is about to break and offer the user a way to keep it.

## Terminology

In this document the feature is referred to as 'Streak Recovery Suggestion Engine'. Alternate phrasing seen elsewhere in the repository includes: streak saver, saving a broken streak, losing a streak.

## Behaviour

The system exposes Streak Recovery Suggestion Engine to the tenant administrator. Behaviour is governed by the linked backlog items below.
