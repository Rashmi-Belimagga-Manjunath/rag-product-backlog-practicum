# Progressive Profiling for New Tenants

Document type: Product Requirements Document | Version: 2.0 | Status: Authoritative | Owner: Group Product Manager | Date: 2026-06-30

Summary: Reduce time-to-value for new tenants by deferring non-essential profile questions out of the first session, and capture partial progress so it is not lost when a user abandons a session.

## Background

Reduce time-to-value for new tenants by deferring non-essential profile questions out of the first session, and capture partial progress so it is not lost when a user abandons a session. This document was raised by Group Product Manager on 2026-06-30 and is version 2.0.

## Goals

- Advance onboarding ramp (Progressive Profiling), currently at status 'in_progress', priority 1.

## Non-goals

- Out of scope for this document: implementation detail, migration planning, and commercial packaging.

## Requirements

- The system shall support onboarding ramp. Status: in_progress. Priority: 1.
  - Tracked as PBI-A-011 (Ask for tax details during the first session), in_progress, 5 points.
  - Tracked as PBI-A-012 (Save partial profile when a user abandons the session), to_do, 8 points.

## Dependencies

- DEP-003: REQ-A-02 depends on the Identity Service for per-session tenant context.
- DEP-001: Progressive Profiling depends on the Identity Service for session-scoped tenant context.
- DEP-004: Bulk Timesheet Approval depends on Progressive Profiling, because the approval screen shows profile completeness and cannot be built before the ramp exists.

## Risks

- Delivery risk is concentrated in the components listed under Dependencies. Third-party components are outside the product team's control.
