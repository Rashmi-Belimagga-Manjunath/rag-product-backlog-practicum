# Decision DEC-A-01

Document: DOC-DEC-A-01
Decided: 2026-07-16
Decided by: Senior Product Manager

## Decision

Move tax collection to day three of the onboarding ramp rather than requiring it during sign-up.

## Rationale

Early testing showed the long sign-up form caused abandonment in the first session. Asking later, once the user has seen value, raised completion of the tax step from 54% to 88%.

## Applies to

FEAT-A-01 (Progressive Profiling).
